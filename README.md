### [V/TV Sleep](https://github.com/vineeth-arch/lgtv)

`lgtv` · Public source

A sleep timer that runs directly on an LG webOS television.<br>
Stores the timer on the TV instead of keeping a phone or computer awake.<br>
Offers preset durations, custom timers and cancellation from the TV interface.<br>
Includes a Mac command-line client and a shared Swift control package.<br>
Helps viewers end late-night watching without an always-on companion server.<br>
Tested on one LG model; Developer Mode is required and the iOS app is planned.

### [V/HOME](https://github.com/vineeth-arch/v-home)

`v-home` · Public source

An ad-free LG webOS launcher with a shared household dashboard.<br>
Organizes favorite apps, folders, HDMI inputs and wallpapers around remote navigation.<br>
Adds household notes, plans, photos and a phone companion.<br>
Integrates V/TV Sleep rather than creating a second shutdown scheduler.<br>
Helps households reach their apps and shared information from one screen.<br>
Requires Developer Mode; its optional Mac helper redirects LG Home on a best-effort basis.
