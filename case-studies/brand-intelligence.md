# Brand Intelligence OS

**RESEARCH / STRATEGY** · Private source · public project narrative

A local-first research system that turns scattered brand signals into a traceable working library.

## The question

Brand research gets fragmented across websites, social channels and advertising libraries. The challenge is preserving what was observed and where it came from.

## The system

The pipeline collects source material and retains raw Markdown, HTML, screenshots and failure records. Structured reports can then be developed from evidence rather than memory.

## Why it helps

Clearer evidence helps strategists compare brands, revisit sources and develop better-grounded questions.

## The craft

Python / research pipelines / evidence design

## Scope and evidence

A documented research system. Blocked or unavailable sources remain explicit gaps; this portfolio does not claim exhaustive coverage.

This narrative is based on repository documentation reviewed on 27 October 2026. It describes the system and its intended value; it is not a new runtime test or a claim of measured business outcomes.
