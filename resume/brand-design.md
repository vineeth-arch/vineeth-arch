## Selected work

### [Brand Intelligence OS](../case-studies/brand-intelligence.md)

A local-first research system that turns scattered brand signals into a traceable working library. Clearer evidence helps strategists compare brands, revisit sources and develop better-grounded questions.

### [V / The studio identity](../case-studies/identity.md)

A personal identity joining brand strategy, design, consulting and software products. Visitors can understand the breadth of the practice while following a consistent visual and narrative structure.

### [V/HOME](../case-studies/v-home.md)

An ad-free LG webOS launcher designed around a shared living room. Households can reach familiar apps and shared information from one intentional home screen.
