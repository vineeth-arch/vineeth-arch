## Selected work

### [Brand Intelligence OS](../case-studies/brand-intelligence.md)

A local-first research system that turns scattered brand signals into a traceable working library. Clearer evidence helps strategists compare brands, revisit sources and develop better-grounded questions.

### [GST Invoice Studio](../case-studies/gst-invoice.md)

An India-focused workspace for invoices, purchase orders and reusable business records. Consultants and studios can prepare consistent paperwork with less repeated entry and clearer document structure.

### [Personal Brain OS](../case-studies/personal-brain.md)

A self-hosted cockpit connecting everyday capture with a Markdown knowledge base. A consistent capture-to-knowledge workflow reduces fragmentation and makes personal context easier to revisit.
